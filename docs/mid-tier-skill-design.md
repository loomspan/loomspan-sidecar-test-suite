# From business processes to reliable skills

This guide retains transferable Phase 2 lessons, not its experiment log. The human
business contract is primary: a practitioner should recognize responsibilities,
decisions and handoffs as a sensible process. Do not fragment work merely to fit a
weaker model. Read [Phase 2 summary](phase2-summary.md) for the observed scope.

## Evidence status

**Demonstrated in a defined scope** means the mechanism was directly checked locally.
**Promising, not established** means observed results support the practice but causal
isolation, repetition or transfer is limited. **Proposed for testing** marks an
unverified remedy. A desirable rule is not automatically a demonstrated model benefit.

| Practice | Status and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Runtime binding and exact publication preserve source values and accepted results | Demonstrated for tested handoffs; preservation does not establish reasoning correctness. |
| Inspect actual consumer requests to verify delivery | Demonstrated on both integrations; schema documentation alone did not ensure visibility. Presence is not compliance. |
| Separate execution, transport and business review | Demonstrated diagnostic value in this process; automated semantic coverage remains limited. |
| Freeze accepted responses separately from disposable run evidence | Demonstrated deterministic replay of four accepted cases on both integrations; tests runtime preservation, not fresh model judgment or broader reasoning reliability. |
| Coherent per-option assessments with shallow generated findings | Promising; useful constraint interactions and valid handoffs were observed, but independent effects of shape and decomposition were not isolated. |
| Complete offers with applicable metadata, units and provenance | Promising; false uncertainty about supplied terms receded, but more data cannot ensure correct use. |
| Explicit service conditions and source-linked owner references | Promising; important conditions survived, while owner-role precision still varied. |
| Separate elapsed activity, billable scope and reference events | Promising; targeted distinctions held in later runs, with some ambiguous wording remaining. |
| Authoritative itemization at the calculation owner | Promising; correct pricing held in confirmation and one unchanged repeat, alongside a simultaneous review-method change. |
| Final review owns all materially published findings | Promising; supported corrections occurred, but review also introduced false corrections in earlier work. |
| Assign capability by coherent business responsibility | Promising; the selected mixed pack passed two fixed-case batches, not a universal model ranking or minimum-cost proof. |
| These practices make new processes easier to author | Proposed for testing on additional examples; not established by one deeply worked reference. |

## Design a process before assigning models

Map what must be retrieved, interpreted, decided, authorized and executed. Give each
skill a coherent judgment and useful handoff. Keep business methods separate from
Framework instructions. No sentence limit or ban on helpful restatement should prevent
a clear explanation. Review methods, schema descriptions and bindings as one contract.

Start with a competent whole tree and reasonable capability. Use lower tiers where
they suffice; use stronger general reasoning or specialist models where the responsibility
needs them. A successful stronger baseline can guide substitutions, but a specific
capability need can justify a mixed pack directly. Neither a whole-tree upgrade nor a
fixed percentage of stronger calls is required. Keep the simpler assignment when it works.

Include human authoring, review and maintenance effort in optimization costs. Further
lower-tier tuning can have diminishing returns. This is not an arbitrary effort cap:
continued investment should serve a clear business or reusable-design objective.

## Keep evidence and calculations with their owners

Let deterministic operations retrieve authoritative records, calculate established
amounts and enforce permissions. Publish a useful calculation breakdown and provenance;
keep maximum exposure separate from actual charges. Let the runtime transport facts
and assemble larger structures instead of asking a model to copy them.

Bound inputs by responsibility while retaining applicable parent terms, dates, units,
source identity and revision. Small inputs that strip essential meaning are not an
improvement. Preserve observation provenance: a later report linked to a work order
is not an observation made in that work order. Compare test conditions and duration
with the failure being investigated before claiming resolution.

Keep quantities attached to activities and reference events. Arrival, setup completion,
restored operation, labor allowance and rental duration are distinct. Use established
activity dates for access calculations; do not invent calendars, start triggers or
completion estimates. Preserve known facts while asking about missing attributes.

## Separate assessment, selection and commitment

Assess feasibility before selecting a strategy: timing, capacity, access, exposure,
authority and prerequisites should interact explicitly. Use responsibility-specific
approval methods where service and procurement differ. A directory entry names a role;
authenticated application checks establish permission. Assessment does not commit.

Intermediate findings are interpretations, not new authority. Comparison must review
all material published content, including deferred alternatives, against original
evidence. Resolve a local assessor's information gap using broader case evidence
before declaring the whole case incomplete. Disagreement alone is not source conflict.

If findings are published unchanged, record governing corrections with their source
and consequence and apply them consistently. An explicit correction can itself be
wrong. Exact publication, a correct primary choice or an empty review-concern list
cannot alone establish business correctness. Preserve genuine unresolved ambiguity.

## Validate what the practitioner will use

Check execution, transport and business outcomes separately. Review technical findings,
all options, operational conditions, owners and final decisions against independent
sources. Count schema corrections separately from retries and valid JSON. Vary meaningful
operating constraints while keeping sources and skills fixed when testing transfer.

Before paid trials, verify that necessary facts and definitions reach actual consumers.
Use provider-free checks for deterministic arithmetic, preservation and permissions.
Freeze a candidate within a trial; do not confuse that control with a ban on justified
future changes. State observed scope, costs, caveats and what remains untested. Repeated
fixed cases support a bounded conclusion, not general reliability or universal causality.
